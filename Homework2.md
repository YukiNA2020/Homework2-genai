# Homework 2: AI Content Monitoring and Generation Workflow

## Objective
The objective of this assignment is to design and document an AI-powered workflow and agentic system that continuously monitors, filters, classifies, and synthesizes the latest developments in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and its business applications. The final output should generate high-quality, domain-relevant thought leadership content suitable for professional platforms such as LinkedIn.

This assignment emphasizes information prioritization, relevance routing, classification, and content generation, within a real-world business decision-making context.

## Assignment Requirements

Design an AI agent that checks the latest AI-related news and information on a daily basis. You may consider store these information and insights in a repository using a vector database or regular database.

### 2. Relevance Routing and Domain Definition (15 points)
Build a relevance router that evaluates whether each piece of information is relevant to:
- Your professional role, and
- Your selected industry and business domain (e.g., finance, consulting, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, SaaS, etc.)

You **must**:
- Clearly define your industry, domain, and target audience
- Choose one or some of the potential relevance criteria, such as:
  - Strategic impact
  - Revenue or cost implications
  - Competitive advantage
  - Operational efficiency
  - Regulatory or governance considerations

---

### 3. Information Classification (15 points)
For all information, news or documents deemed relevant, design and implement an AI-based classification mechanism that groups information into several meaningful, business-relevant categories.

Example categories (you may define your own):
- Strategy and Executive Decision-Making
- Product and Service Innovation
- Infrastructure, Models, and Platforms
- Governance, Ethics, and Regulation
- Industry-Specific AI Use Cases

---

### 4. LinkedIn content research (15 points)
Choose 2–5 KOLs (Key Opinion Leaders) you admire, reverse engineer their content.

Document:
- How they open (hooks)
- How they structure (scannable formatting)
- How they build credibility (data, cases, opinions)
- How they invite engagement (questions, provocations)
- Their style: concise vs narrative, contrarian vs instructional, etc.

Output: a short “LinkedIn post style and anatomy” checklist that you’ll embed into your workflow as a content generation guideline and evaluation rubric.

---

### 5. LinkedIn Content Generation (20 points)
Choose 2-3 categories in step 3. For each category:
Generate professional LinkedIn contents with both text and image using the guideline in step 4:
- Translates AI developments into clear business implications
- Demonstrates depth of analysis and strategic thinking
- Is concise, engaging, and suitable for executives, founders, or investors

You **must specify**:
- Target audience for each post
- Tone and positioning (e.g., thought leadership, insight-sharing, executive briefing)

---

### 6. Workflow, Prompt Optimization and Progress Report (20 points)
Prepare a structured progress report documenting:
- Workflow architecture and design logic
- Key challenges encountered
- Workflow and prompt optimization progress, explaining What was improved, How the changes enhanced content quality, efficiency, or consistency etc
- Lessons learned
- Future opportunities for improvement or automation
